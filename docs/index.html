Everything a shared trip needs

Halvsies is a tiny app you drop into a chat. Everyone in that chat shares one ledger, synced through the messenger itself — end-to-end encrypted wherever your chat is.

Pay in one tap

Payment links for PayPal, bunq, Monzo, Cash App, UPI, Revolut, Wise, Venmo and more — most with the amount already filled in. Plus SEPA and Czech bank-transfer QR codes, copyable IBAN details and crypto addresses.

Few transfers

Balances are simplified into a short list of payments that settles the whole group.

Split it your way

Evenly, by shares (two people in a double room?), or by exact amounts. Your own share shows on every expense.

No accounts, no server

Nothing to sign up for and nothing leaves the chat. The ledger travels as chat messages between the people in it.

Works offline

Add expenses on the plane. Changes merge automatically when everyone's back online.

Lives in the chat

New expenses and settled debts are announced in the conversation, so nobody has to ask "did you pay me yet?"

How to use it

From download to settled up in five steps.

  1. Get the app into your chat. Download halvsies.xdc and send it into your group chat like any file attachment.
  2. Set up the group. The first person to open it names the split ("Rome trip") and picks the currency.
  3. Everyone taps it to join. Chat members join automatically when they open the app, using their chat name. Friends who aren't in the chat can be added by hand under Me → Group settings → Members.
  4. Add expenses. Tap + Add expense, enter what and how much, who paid and who's in. Split evenly, by shares or by exact amounts. The chat gets a short note for each one.
  5. Settle up. The Balances tab shows a short list of transfers that clears everything. Tap a debt to pay it with the payee's payment methods, then Mark as paid — the balance updates for everyone. Add your own payment details in the Me tab so others can pay you the same way.
